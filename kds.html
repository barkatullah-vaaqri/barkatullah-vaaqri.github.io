How it works

Three moves. Every service.

From the till or the Diner app to the pass, without a slip of paper.

  1. 1

    Tickets land at their station

    Orders from the till and the Diner app arrive at the station that cooks them, with allergies and modifiers on the ticket.

  2. 2

    Start, then mark it ready

    Cooks start a ticket and mark it ready at the station, with a moment to undo. Timers show what is running over.

  3. 3

    The pass brings it together

    The expo screen shows each order across stations and releases it when every station is ready.

Before you book a demo

Straight answers to the questions that come before a demo. Anything else, ask us on the call.

Book a free demo
Do I need special kitchen screens?

No. The kitchen display runs in the browser on the tablets or screens you already have.

Do I still need a ticket printer?

You choose per station. Each station can run on a kitchen screen or a printer.

What if a screen loses its connection?

The board says it is offline straight away and reconciles the tickets when it reconnects.

How do changes reach the cook?

Amended items are marked CHANGED and new ones NEW on the ticket, and a cancellation arrives as its own ticket.

What happens on the free demo?

It is a 30-minute call with you and your manager, a walkthrough of one service on your own menu, then a plan for your first site. You decide from there.

Can we try it before we commit?

Yes. The pilot runs for thirty days on one site, with no setup fee, and you can cancel any month.
