The waste story · one ingredient, one week

Waste isn’t decided at the bin. It’s decided at the order.

Same kitchen. Same menu. Same paneer. One week run on habit and a Sunday count, one run on VAAQRI. Here is what ends up in the bin.

On habit

Ordered from memory. Counted on Sunday. Binned on Thursday.

  1. MonLast week’s order arrives again. Nobody checks the shelf first.
  2. TueA busy service. How much paneer is left? Nobody knows.
  3. WedIt feels low, so another 2 kg goes on the order.+2 kg
  4. ThuThe pack at the back turns. Straight in the bin.1.2 kg binned
  5. SunCounted from memory. The waste never shows up.

With VAAQRI

Counted as it sells. Used before it turns. Ordered to match.

  1. MonScanned at the door. Use by Thursday, on the record.
  2. TueEvery paneer tikka takes 180 g off the shelf as it sells.2.2 kg left
  3. WedTwo days to go, so it tops the use-first list. Order: 0 kg.0 kg ordered
  4. ThuPaneer special on the board. Sold before it turns.Nothing binned
  5. SunNo Sunday count. The shelf figure is already today’s.

Questions

Before you book a demo

Straight answers to the questions that come before a demo. Anything else, ask us on the call.

Book a free demo
What does it cost to try?

The pilot runs for thirty days on one site, with no setup fee, and you can cancel any month. Ask us about pricing for your sites on the demo call.

Do I need to keep my existing till?

No. VAAQRI is the till. Staff orders, QR orders, the kitchen screen, deliveries and stock share one system.

What hardware do I need?

Nothing special. VAAQRI runs in the browser on the tablets and phones you already have.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Guests scan the code at the table and order and pay from their own phone, with no app and no account.

How do we get started?

Book a demo. It is a 30-minute call with you and your manager, a walkthrough of one service on your own menu, then a plan for your first site. You decide from there.

Does it work for more than one site?

Yes. Every order, ticket, delivery and stock line is kept per site, so you see all of them in one view, with the sites that need attention first.

How does VAAQRI handle allergens?

Each dish records which of the fourteen regulated allergens it contains, and staff and guests see it when they order. Every allergen has one of three states: contains, confirmed free from (dated), or not checked.

What do you do with the details I send?

We use them only to arrange your demo. No newsletter, no sharing, and this site sets no analytics or advertising cookies.
